===============================================================================
NIGHTINGALE CLINICAL DETERMINISTIC HIGHLIGHTING SPECIFICATION v1.0
===============================================================================

1. ONTOLOGY & RISK TIER MAPPING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
System enforces 5 distinct functional categories with fixed risk floors and 
pastel UI themes to prevent alert fatigue:

[CRITICAL / HIGH RISK]
- Categories: Critical Symptoms, Severe Vitals, Anaphylactic Reactions.
- Terms: chest pain, shortness of breath, anaphylaxis, severe respiratory distress, SPO2 < 90%, BP > 180/110.
- Color Token: Soft Red (bg-red-500/15 text-red-700 border-red-300)
- Risk Floor: CRITICAL

[WARNING / MEDIUM RISK]
- Categories: Abnormal Labs, High Fever, Moderate Vitals.
- Terms: hyperpyrexia, fever, elevated WBC, acute confusion, tachycardic.
- Color Token: Soft Amber (bg-amber-500/15 text-amber-800 border-amber-300)
- Risk Floor: MEDIUM

[INFO / MEDICATIONS & ALLERGIES]
- Categories: Active Prescriptions, Known Allergies.
- Terms: penicillin, aspirin, metformin, amlodipine, lisinopril, insulin.
- Color Token: Soft Green (bg-green-500/15 text-green-800 border-green-300)
- Risk Floor: LOW

[UNRESOLVED ACTIONS]
- Categories: Pending Diagnostics, Outstanding Lab Orders, Follow-ups.
- Terms: needs lab order, pending echo, follow-up required, order STAT CT.
- Color Token: Soft Blue (bg-blue-500/15 text-blue-800 border-blue-300)
- Risk Floor: ACTION_REQUIRED

[PATIENT INSIGHTS]
- Categories: Patient Concerns, Subjective Complaints.
- Terms: nausea, insomnia, anxious, dizziness, fatigue.
- Color Token: Soft Purple (bg-purple-500/15 text-purple-800 border-purple-300)
- Risk Floor: LOW

2. CONTEXT & NEGATION RULES (NegEx Principles)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Negation Filter: Skip highlighting if target phrase is preceded within 1-5 words
  by negation triggers: "no", "denies", "negative for", "without", "ruled out".
  Example: "Patient denies chest pain" -> "chest pain" MUST NOT BE HIGHLIGHTED.
- Historical Demotion: Phrases preceded by "history of", "past history", or 
  "family history of" are automatically demoted to INFO / LOW risk tier.

3. DEDUPLICATION & VISUAL FATIGUE REDUCTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Single-Match Rule: Only the FIRST occurrence of a target entity in a single 
  care note body gets auto-highlighted. Subsequent duplicate words remain plain text.
- Overlap Protection (Longest-Match-First): If both "pain" and "chest pain" 
  match, highlight "chest pain" as a single unified span.
- Low-Signal Blacklist: Suppress standalone ambiguous terms ("pain", "cough", 
  "normal", "well") unless qualified by severe/specific modifiers.

4. DUAL-SOURCE PROVENANCE & OVERRIDE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Auto-Highlights: Rendered with source="MODEL", showing a subtle AI/sparkle badge.
- Manual Highlights: Created by Clinicians/Staff (source="HUMAN"), taking absolute
  visual and semantic precedence over any overlapping auto-highlight.
