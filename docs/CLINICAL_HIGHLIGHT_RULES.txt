NIGHTINGALE CLINICAL DETERMINISTIC HIGHLIGHTING SPECIFICATION v1.2
===============================================================================

1. ONTOLOGY & RISK TIER MAPPING
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
System enforces 5 distinct functional categories with fixed risk floors and 
pastel UI themes to prevent alert fatigue:

[CRITICAL / HIGH RISK]
- Categories: Critical Symptoms, Severe Vitals, Anaphylactic Reactions.
- Terms: chest pain, shortness of breath, anaphylaxis, severe respiratory distress, SPO2 < 90%, BP > 180/110.
- Color Token: Soft Red (bg-red-500/15 text-red-700 border-red-300)
- Risk Floor: CRITICAL

[WARNING / MEDIUM RISK]
- Categories: Abnormal Labs, High Fever, Moderate Vitals.
- Terms: hyperpyrexia, fever, elevated WBC, acute confusion, tachycardic.
- Color Token: Soft Amber (bg-amber-500/15 text-amber-800 border-amber-300)
- Risk Floor: MEDIUM

[INFO / MEDICATIONS & ALLERGIES]
- Categories: Active Prescriptions, Known Allergies.
- Terms: penicillin, aspirin, metformin, amlodipine, lisinopril, insulin.
- Color Token: Soft Green (bg-green-500/15 text-green-800 border-green-300)
- Risk Floor: LOW

[UNRESOLVED ACTIONS]
- Categories: Pending Diagnostics, Outstanding Lab Orders, Follow-ups.
- Terms: needs lab order, pending echo, follow-up required, order STAT CT.
- Color Token: Soft Blue (bg-blue-500/15 text-blue-800 border-blue-300)
- Risk Floor: ACTION_REQUIRED

[PATIENT INSIGHTS]
- Categories: Patient Concerns, Subjective Complaints.
- Terms: nausea, insomnia, anxious, dizziness, fatigue.
- Color Token: Soft Purple (bg-purple-500/15 text-purple-800 border-purple-300)
- Risk Floor: LOW

2. CONTEXT & NEGATION RULES (NegEx Principles)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Negation Filter: Skip highlighting if target phrase is preceded within 1-5 words
  by negation triggers: "no", "denies", "negative for", "without", "ruled out".
  Example: "Patient denies chest pain" -> "chest pain" MUST NOT BE HIGHLIGHTED.
- Historical Demotion: Phrases preceded by "history of", "past history", or 
  "family history of" are automatically demoted to INFO / LOW risk tier.

2a. CONTEXT RULE CLARIFICATIONS (v1.1, implemented in keyword-highlights.ts)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Window: a trigger applies when it is one of the five counted words preceding
  the phrase, inside the same sentence (boundaries: . ! ? ; newline; a decimal
  point inside a number is not a boundary).
- Counted words exclude list connectors (or, and, nor, /, &) and words that
  belong to other lexicon mentions, so one trigger covers a coordinated list:
  "denies chest pain, hyperpyrexia, or new allergy" negates all three.
- Scope terminators end a trigger's reach: but, however, although, though, yet,
  except, still, which, whereas, and a comma followed by "and". "No fever but
  chest pain" affirms chest pain; "fever without hyperpyrexia, and nausea" affirms
  nausea, while ", or" keeps a negated list going ("denies X, Y, or Z").
- Negation dominates: "no history of chest pain" is NEGATED, not HISTORICAL.
- Inflections: "denied" and "deny" are negation triggers alongside "denies".
- Hypothetical / safety-net demotion (new in v1.1): phrases preceded by "if",
  "should", "would", "unless", "in case of", "red flag(s)", "warning signs",
  "warning symptoms", "safety net" / "safety-net" describe what to watch for,
  not a current finding, and are demoted to INFO.
- Selection: one auto-highlight per term per note. The first AFFIRMED mention
  wins; if none is affirmed, the first demoted mention is kept at INFO. Negated
  mentions are never highlighted.
- Safety bias: context rules only ever demote inside the tight five-word window.
  When the window does not settle it, the engine keeps the higher tier. Over-
  alerting is the accepted failure direction; silent demotion is not.

- Resolution demotion (new in v1.2): a phrase IMMEDIATELY followed by a
  past-tense resolution verb is HISTORICAL: "the fever resolved four days
  ago", "chest pain has settled", "now resolved", "fully subsided". Only
  "resolved", "settled" and "subsided", optionally after has / had / have /
  now / fully / completely. Not demoted: present tense ("pain that settles
  with rest", a typical exertional pattern), negation ("has not resolved"),
  and anything further away ("chest pain, which resolved after GTN"). Added
  because "The fever resolved" was the only false positive of the risk floor
  in the 2026-11 evaluation (docs/EVALUATION.md B4c).

2b. SEVERE VITALS (v1.1 implementation of the section 1 vitals terms)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- SpO2 / saturations / sats followed by a value below 90% -> CRITICAL.
- BP / blood pressure systolic > 180 or diastolic > 110 -> CRITICAL.

2c. KNOWN DIVERGENCE (open product decision)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The runtime keeps the pre-v1.1 tiers for existing lexicon entries: fever and
allergy are HIGH, hyperpyrexia is CRITICAL. Section 1 lists fever and
hyperpyrexia as MEDIUM and allergies under INFO. Re-tiering changes which
findings the agent guardrail treats as non-downgradable, so it is left to the
clinical product owner rather than changed in an engineering pass.

3. DEDUPLICATION & VISUAL FATIGUE REDUCTION
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Single-Match Rule: Only the FIRST occurrence of a target entity in a single 
  care note body gets auto-highlighted. Subsequent duplicate words remain plain text.
- Overlap Protection (Longest-Match-First): If both "pain" and "chest pain" 
  match, highlight "chest pain" as a single unified span.
- Low-Signal Blacklist: Suppress standalone ambiguous terms ("pain", "cough", 
  "normal", "well") unless qualified by severe/specific modifiers.

4. DUAL-SOURCE PROVENANCE & OVERRIDE
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Auto-Highlights: Rendered with source="MODEL", showing a subtle AI/sparkle badge.
- Manual Highlights: Created by Clinicians/Staff (source="HUMAN"), taking absolute
  visual and semantic precedence over any overlapping auto-highlight.
